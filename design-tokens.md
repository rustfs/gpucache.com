## Color

- Brand Blue: `#006CFF`
- Brand Orange: `#FF6A00`
- Ink / Black: `#050505`
- Paper / White: `#FFFFFF`
- Soft Paper: `#F6F6F2`
- Muted Text: `#5F646D`
- Border: `#D9E0EA`

## Typography

- Headlines: Georgia / Times-compatible serif stack, heavy weight, tight negative tracking.
- Body/UI: Inter / system sans stack.
- Hero headline: large editorial scale, black on white or white on blue.
- Body copy: 18–22px, 1.55–1.65 line-height.

## Spacing

- 8px base spacing system.
- Desktop page max width: 1200px.
- Section vertical spacing: 128–160px.
- Card/block padding: 32–48px.
- Grid gap: 24px.

## Shape

- The Figma-like RustFS style uses sharp editorial geometry more than soft SaaS cards.
- Primary blocks use square corners or very small radius.
- Buttons use pill radius only for call-to-action controls.

## Layout Rules

- Strong blue/white/black contrast.
- Orange only as emphasis/accent, not as a decorative gradient.
- Avoid glassmorphism, dark neon gradients, artificial dashboards, and unrelated metrics.
- Keep the layout poster-like and editorial: large headings, disciplined whitespace, clear blocks.
